## Front matter reference

A few commonly used fields, all optional except `title` and `date`:

- `categories: [A, B]` — up to two categories, shown on the post card and used by
  the Categories tab
- `tags: [x, y, z]` — as many as you like, used by the Tags tab
- `pin: true` — pins the post to the top of listings
- `image: /path/to/image.png` — a thumbnail shown on the post card
- `math: true` — enables LaTeX math rendering for this post
- `mermaid: true` — enables Mermaid diagram rendering for this post

## Filename convention

Every post file must be named exactly `YYYY-MM-DD-some-title.md` and live inside
`_posts/`. The date in the filename should match the `date:` in front matter.
