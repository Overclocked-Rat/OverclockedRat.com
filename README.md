## Why This Site Exists

### Blog
To put my thougts on "paper" for various topics, things that i find interesting or may be usefull down the line for others.

### Tools
I was sick of trying to find reliable online converters and tools that either weren't riddled with ads or would attempt to extort me for money after i have allready uploaded the files.

The piece of mind of knowing some random stranger isnt keeping copies of my files is also a nice benefit as all the tools on this site are run client side exclusively.

### Emulators
While they werent part of my original plan, the idea grew on me and i plan to continue adding more emulators.
